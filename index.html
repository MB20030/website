A little about me

Curious about how things work.
Motivated to build them.

Hi, I’m Moritz. I study Economics at the University of St. Gallen and enjoy working where analytical thinking meets hands-on execution.

Alongside university, I’m co-building e-SKI (opens in a new tab), an electric ski-touring assist system, with a focus on business development, fundraising, and strategy. I’ve also built AI workflows at a sustainability startup and worked on business intelligence in healthcare.

My bachelor thesis explores how Swiss electoral rules shape the representation of smaller parties. Outside work and university, I’m involved in local politics, enjoy the mountains, and like learning languages.

Always happy to exchange ideas about economics, startups, and useful technology. Feel free to reach out.
